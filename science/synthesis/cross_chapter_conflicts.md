Each record retains both source locators, why they differ, classification, canonical treatment and verification requirement. SOURCE_MODEL, SOURCE_ERA_STATEMENT, SOURCE_SCALE and VERIFY_QUANT restrictions in the passages remain binding. No runtime value has been chosen or averaged.
