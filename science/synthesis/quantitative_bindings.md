## Binding contract

Each stable `Q.nnn` record identifies a source file/section and unique row or prose selector, canonical target(s), parameter field, applicability, inherited source wording/status and a hold. Multiple targets describe the same observation or its participants, not permission to assign the value to every target. Compound source rows must be split into separately qualified observations on conversion; values cannot be averaged.

A future binding supports `binding_id`, `target_id`, `property`, `source_locator`, `source_status`, `source_approval`, `value_reference`, `unit_reference`, `context`, `measurement_definition`, `uncertainty`, `conflict_ids`, `real_value_reference`, `display_value_reference` and `promotion_status`. Missing context/status is explicitly unknown. Descriptive words such as “measured example” are retained without manufacturing an uppercase source tag. Every binding inherits organism/cell type/state/phase, compartment, temperature, medium, assay, uncertainty and source lineage from its selected passage; missing fields require evidence.

`CTX.generic_mammalian_traversal` is a **PROJECT REFERENCE CONTEXT** for the quantitative traversal example. It does not define `cell.size`, a sphere, volume, mitochondrial inventory or a universal mammalian cell. See [ontology](ontology.md).

172 binding records cover every quantitative data-table row and the substantive prose constraints in files 01–06. Repeated observations in different source passages remain separate bindings to the same canonical identity. Files README/00/07 govern every record; their bibliographic lists are not numerical biological entries.
