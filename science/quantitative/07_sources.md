# Quantitative source registry

## Primary synthesis

### Cell Biology by the Numbers
Ron Milo and Rob Phillips.

Main site:

https://book.bionumbers.org/

The book explicitly organizes biological numeracy around physical axes such as:
- size and geometry
- concentrations and absolute numbers
- energies and forces
- rates and durations
- information/fidelity

It also emphasizes Fermi-style order-of-magnitude reasoning and uncertainty rather than false precision.
